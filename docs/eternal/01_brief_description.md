# 1. Brief Description

Rent deposits that earn yield and come back automatically — held on Solana, not by the landlord.

## Optional second sentence (if the field allows)

Tenants lock their deposit in a Solana escrow that earns yield for them, landlords approve release in one click or raise an evidenced claim, and unresolved claims go to a DAO jury — with the burden of proof on the landlord.
