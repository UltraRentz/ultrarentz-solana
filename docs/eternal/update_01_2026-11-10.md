# Builder Update #1 — Day 1: UltraRentz goes native on Solana

UltraRentz turns rent deposits into onchain escrows that earn yield for the tenant and release automatically at move-out. Today we started the 4-week sprint by rebuilding the protocol in Rust (Anchor 0.32).

**Shipped today**
- Core Solana program written. It covers deposit lock, rolling-tenancy notice, one-click landlord release, evidenced landlord claims, DAO juror voting and a permissionless settle crank.
- Automatic release: if the landlord raises no claim inside the claim window, anyone can trigger settlement and the tenant gets the full deposit back.
- Fair disputes: the burden of proof is on the landlord. If quorum isn't reached, the tenant wins. The landlord is awarded the lower median of the juror votes. A tenant who has left the country is never defaulted against.
- Yield is paid to the tenant. The protocol fee is charged on yield only, never on principal.
- The program compiles, and 7 unit tests pass for the payout split and median voting, including the edge case where the vault holds less than principal.

**Market decision**
- England is our test market and runs on test tokens only, because statutory deposit protection schemes apply there.
- Ireland is our first real market. Its 2015 deposit protection scheme was never commenced, so landlords still hold deposits themselves. UltraRentz fills that gap.
